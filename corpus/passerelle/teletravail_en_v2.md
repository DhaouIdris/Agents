# Remote work — Passerelle policy, version 2

Entirely synthetic training document. Fictional organisation: Passerelle Agency.

## Scope

This version is published on 1 April 2026 and applies from that date.
It replaces version 1 dated 1 September 2025.
The French version of the same policy has the identifier teletravail-fr-v2.

## Number of days

A staff member may request up to two remote-work days per week.
This limit does not constitute automatic approval.
The line manager assesses compatibility with the team's duties.

## Procedure

The request specifies the desired days and the relevant period.
The manager must approve it before the requested period starts.
An incomplete request must be clarified before submission.

## Equipment

Equipment needs require a separate request.
This policy specifies neither a reimbursement amount nor a delivery deadline.
